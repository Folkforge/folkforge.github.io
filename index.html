DISCOVER THE SECRETS OF THE ELMDEN

The Elmder Keeper is an atmospheric first-person adventure set in a mysterious coastal landscape. As the newly appointed Keeper, you must unravel the mysteries of the Elmden Tree and restore balance to a world on the edge of darkness.

THE WORLD

A Living, Breathing Island

Explore a hauntingly beautiful island environment that changes with time and weather. The island of Elmden is a character itself - revealing new secrets as you progress through your journey.

  • Rugged coastal cliffs and mysterious tidal pools
  • Ancient forests with towering dead trees
  • Abandoned cabins and hidden pathways
  • Dynamic day/night cycle with stunning atmospheric effects

THE STORY

After receiving a mysterious invitation, you arrive at the remote island of Elmden to assume the role of the new Keeper. The previous caretaker has vanished, leaving behind cryptic messages about a growing darkness threatening the ancient Elmder Tree - the mystical heart of the island.

As you explore the island and interact with its few remaining inhabitants, you'll uncover the truth about the Keeper's role and the ancient pact that binds the human world to the mysterious forces of the Elmden. Your choices will determine whether the balance can be restored or if the darkness will consume everything.

ABOUT STUDIO FOLKFORGE

Studio Folkforge is an independent game development studio dedicated to creating atmospheric, narrative-driven experiences that blend mystery, folklore, and player agency. The Elmder Keeper is our debut title, representing our passion for environmental storytelling and immersive gameplay.

Our team combines expertise in environmental design, narrative development, and atmospheric gameplay to create unique gaming experiences that stay with players long after they've put down the controller.
